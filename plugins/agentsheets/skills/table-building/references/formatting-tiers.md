## Formatting tiers for a financial statement

Format a P&L in five tiers:

- **Tier 1 — Detail lines**, such as Subscription revenue: indent; no bold or background.
- **Tier 2 — Structural totals**, such as Total revenue or Total operating expenses: flush left
  and bold; no background.
- **Tier 3 — Mid-tier subtotals**, such as Total non-headcount expense: indent and bold; no
  background.
- **Tier 4 — Calculated milestones**, such as Gross profit, Operating profit/(loss), or Net
  profit/(loss): flush left, bold, and use a `teal` background.
- **Tier 5 — Percentages**, such as Gross margin % or Operating margin %: flush left and italic,
  not bold. Use a `yellow` background.

Within one artifact, use `teal` for every tier 4 line and `yellow` for every tier 5 line. The fill
palette has a fixed set of names and no lightness setting. Apply styles in the same form used by the
table menus. The table-building manual explains the formatting tool and batching rules.
